1.

Introduction

Welcome to Routiq.

Routiq is a personal habit-tracking application designed and developed by Offscript ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, and use the Routiq mobile application on Android and iOS devices.

We value your privacy as a foundational design principle. Routiq is intentionally architected to minimize the collection and transmission of personal information. The application operates on a local-first model, keeping your habits, daily routines, and personal records confined to your physical device.

This policy describes the categories of data stored by the application, how that data is utilized, how it is protected within the mobile operating system sandbox, and the rights and choices available to you.

2.

Information We Handle

Routiq allows you to define custom routines, log daily activities, analyze consistency over time, and configure personal reminders. All data processed by Routiq is created directly by you during your use of the application.

Depending on how you configure and use Routiq, the information stored locally includes:

  • Habit Definitions: Habit titles, descriptions, assigned categories (e.g., Health, Productivity, Mindfulness), color identifiers, icon symbols, custom display ordering, and active or archived status.
  • Schedules & Frequencies: Configured days of the week for habit execution, effective scheduling dates, and versioned schedule updates.
  • Tracking Goals & Progress Records: Daily completion status for binary (Yes/No) habits, and numeric entries with custom units (e.g., minutes, pages, glasses) and precision settings for quantitative habits.
  • Daily Logs & Historical Records: Calendar date timestamps and daily completion snapshots used to calculate streaks, completion rates, and weekly performance highlights.
  • Reminder Configurations: Scheduled alarm times (hour and minute), target weekdays, reminder text, and notification identifiers.
  • Application Preferences: Local interface settings, such as selected visual theme mode (light or dark appearance).

This information is processed exclusively to provide the core habit-management, progress-tracking, and reminder functionality of the application.

Information Routiq Does Not Collect

Routiq does not collect, transmit, or process your legal name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, photos, camera feed, precise location, financial accounts, or device identifiers.

3.

How Your Information Is Stored

Routiq is built as an offline-first, local-first application:

  • Local SQLite Database: In the current version of the application, all habits, schedules, logs, and settings are written directly to a structured SQLite database (personal_tracker.db) located inside the application's private, sandboxed filesystem on your device.
  • No User Account or Registration: You do not need to register an account, sign in with an email address, create credentials, or connect a social media profile. You can launch the application and track your habits immediately without authentication.
  • No Application Backend: Offscript does not maintain a remote database, cloud server, or web API for Routiq. Your tracking records are never uploaded to Offscript servers.

Operating System Cloud Backups

While Routiq does not operate an application server or custom cloud synchronization service, your mobile operating system may back up the application's local storage if you have enabled system-level device backups:

  • Android Auto Backup: If enabled in your Android device settings, Google Drive may automatically back up application data files into your personal Google Drive storage.
  • iOS iCloud Backup: If enabled on your Apple device, iOS may include Routiq's local sandboxed data container as part of your scheduled, encrypted iCloud device backup.

These backups are managed entirely by Google or Apple, linked to your private personal account, governed by the respective operating system's privacy policies, and can be disabled at any time through your device's system settings.

4.

Notifications

Routiq allows you to configure optional reminder notifications to prompt you to complete your habits at specific times and on specific days.

How Reminders Work:

  • 100% Local Scheduling: Reminders are scheduled directly on your device through the operating system's native alarm and notification scheduler (such as Android AlarmManager / NotificationManager and iOS UserNotifications framework).
  • No Push Notification Servers: Routiq does not use remote push notification services, external message relays, or cloud delivery brokers. Remote push tokens are neither generated nor transmitted.
  • Permission Management: When you first enable a reminder, Routiq requests device notification permission. Notification permission is entirely optional. If you decline, you can still use every habit-tracking and analytics feature in the app. You can modify or revoke this permission at any time through your device's system settings.
5.

Information Sharing and Third-Party Services

Offscript does not share, sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal habit records, analytics, or reminder data.

Based on our verified source code and technical architecture:

  • No Analytics SDKs: Routiq contains no behavioral tracking, user telemetry, or third-party analytics libraries (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or Amplitude).
  • No Crash Reporting SDKs: No external automated crash reporting frameworks (such as Sentry, Crashlytics, or Bugsnag) are initialized in the application.
  • No Advertising Networks: Routiq contains zero advertising SDKs, ad measurement tools, or behavioral profiling trackers.
  • No Remote API Integrations: Routiq does not make network calls to external APIs, web servers, or cloud microservices during normal operation.

Because there is no transmission of tracking data, your habit information remains private to your device.

6.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because Routiq stores your data locally on your device, you maintain full control over data retention and permanent deletion:

  • In-App Habit Management: You can edit habits, change schedules, clear daily quantitative or binary logs, or archive habits at any time from within the application interface.
  • Clearing Application Storage: You can permanently erase all stored habits, history, categories, and settings by navigating to your device's Settings → Apps → Routiq → Storage and selecting Clear Data (or Clear Storage).
  • Uninstalling the Application: Uninstalling Routiq removes the application sandbox, permanently deleting the local SQLite database file and cancelling all pending local notification alarms from your device.
  • Cloud Backups Deletion: If you use operating system cloud backups (Google Drive on Android or iCloud on iOS), you can manage or delete backed-up application copies directly through your Google or Apple account storage management settings.
7.

Data Security

The security of your information relies on the physical protection of your device and the security architecture of the underlying mobile operating system:

  • Application Sandboxing: Both Android and iOS isolate Routiq within a dedicated application sandbox. Under standard operating system security models, other installed applications cannot access Routiq's private files or its SQLite database.
  • Absence of Network Transmission: Because Routiq does not transmit your habit logs across the internet to remote servers, your data is not susceptible to server-side data breaches, remote credential theft, or interception during transmission.

User Security Recommendations: We recommend protecting your device with a secure screen lock (PIN, biometric authentication, or password), avoiding rooting or jailbreaking your device, and keeping your device's operating system updated with the latest security patches.

8.

Children's Privacy

Routiq is a general-audience productivity utility intended for personal habit tracking and self-improvement.

Routiq is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in applicable European Union jurisdictions), and we do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children. Because the application requires no account registration, collects no personal identifiers, and transmits no information across the internet, no personal data from any user—regardless of age—is gathered, centralized, or shared.

If a parent or guardian has questions regarding their child's use of Routiq on a family device, they may contact us using the information provided below.
